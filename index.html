About Sai Aranya

Ramaniyam Sai Aranya is a serene residential community in Nerkundram, Chennai, developed by Ramaniyam Real Estates Pvt. Ltd. Spread across 3.14 acres, the project offers thoughtfully designed 1, 2, and 3 BHK homes built for space, comfort, and everyday wellness. With unit sizes starting from 807 sq. ft. up to 2251 sq. ft., residents can choose layouts that match their lifestyle. The gated community houses 488 families across three towers and includes 18 lifestyle amenities focused on health, leisure, and community living. Surrounded by greenery yet well connected to the city, Sai Aranya creates a balanced environment where modern living meets peaceful surroundings and a strong sense of belonging.
